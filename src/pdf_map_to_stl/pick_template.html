Position points (optional)

One or two points with their latitude and longitude, so the reader knows where the map is and can use it with a navigation app. On the model each point gets tall pins (point 1: one pin, point 2: two) and its coordinates in Braille. Click "Add a point", then click the place on the map, then type its coordinates, as a web map gives them (44.51234, 11.34567) or in degrees, minutes and seconds.
